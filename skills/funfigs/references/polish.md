# Writing polish

We edit the supplied wording without inventing facts, commitments, results or quotations. We preserve meaning, audience, technical identifiers and the requested channel.

We replace generic introductions, inflated claims, repeated conclusions and abstract business language with specific statements. We remove unnecessary headings and filler, but retain qualifications that express real uncertainty. We do not treat a stylistic preference as proof that text was AI-written.

We use UK English for prose and follow the project's established spelling for code, APIs and identifiers. We apply the live global writing preferences, not a copied private profile. If explicitly supplied or requested, local voice examples may live in `.local/writing-style.md` at the Funfigs root. We resolve the real skill path to locate it, read only for this task, and never copy private examples into tracked guides.

For messages we preserve warmth and the user's intended degree of directness. For engineering documents we make the decision, evidence, ownership and next action clear. We do not impose first-person wording on verbatim quotations or change the identity of a speaker.

We return the polished text directly unless an explanation or comparison was requested. Drafting never authorises sending, posting or changing a shared document.
